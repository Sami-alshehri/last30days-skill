**🚨 Breaches & Attacks**
- WorkOS reported a security breach isolated to internal GTM systems involving the ARTEX autonomous AI agent framework, confirming production systems and customer data were not accessed. <https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50345712>
- A cyberattack targeted supermarket freezers across Europe, including Belgium and Sweden, raising temperatures to 50°C and destroying food inventory. <https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/supermarket-freezers-across-europe-struck-in-second-crippling-cyberattack-hackers-raise-temperatures-to-50-degrees-celsius-in-belgium-and-sweden-destroying-food-stocks>
- SuperValu removed chilled and frozen food from 13 stores across Ireland following a cyberattack on store refrigeration systems. <https://www.irishtimes.com/technology/data-security/2026/11/02/supervalu-removes-frozen-and-chilled-food-from-13-stores-after-cyber-attack-on-fridges/>
- Swedish grocery giants ICA and COOP were impacted by the broader European freezer cyberattack wave. <https://twitter.com/InsiderGeo/status/2117164877701595165>
- Ford announced its "Privacy Promise" for connected vehicles, though user vehicle data sharing remains configured as opt-out via in-vehicle settings. <https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/articles/2026/introducing-the-ford-privacy-promise>
Why it matters: Cyberattacks directly compromising physical IoT infrastructure like commercial refrigeration threaten food supply chains and cause immediate physical inventory losses, while autonomous AI frameworks represent an evolving vector in enterprise network breaches.
